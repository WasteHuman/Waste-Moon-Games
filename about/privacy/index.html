Main Information

WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

The Game has no registration or accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. Your game progress (coins, scores, upgrades, selected skins and balls, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

ANALYTICS

To understand how players use the Game and to fix bugs, we use the analytics service AppMetrica (Yandex). It may automatically receive technical data: the device advertising ID, device model and operating system version, language, IP address and the approximate region derived from it, session and in-game event data, and crash reports. This data is processed on Yandex servers, including in Russia and Finland. Yandex Privacy Policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

ADVERTISING

The Game shows ads provided by the Yandex advertising network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK). The ad network may use the device advertising ID, IP address and technical device data to serve ads and measure their performance. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. Yandex Ads Privacy Policy: https://yandex.com/legal/international_ads_privacy_policy

HOW WE USE DATA

The data available to us is used only to operate and improve the Game, fix bugs and show ads. We do not sell personal data.

SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES

Data may be received only by the third-party services listed in this policy, and only as far as they need it to work. We may also disclose data when required by law.

STORAGE AND SECURITY

Local data stays on your device, so its safety depends on how well your device is protected. Data held by third-party services is stored under those services' own rules.

CHILDREN

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on the law where you live, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent to its processing. To do so, or to ask a question, email us at the address in the Contact section. You can turn off personalized ads in your device settings.
